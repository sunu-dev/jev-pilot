# Community conduct

Treat people respectfully. Discuss the behavior and evidence of a change, not a contributor's identity or ability. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, credential disclosure and spam are not welcome.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation. To report a concern privately, contact the repository owner through a private channel they provide; for security vulnerabilities use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting when enabled. Do not publish sensitive details in a public issue.

Questions, corrections, documentation improvements and reports of negative results are welcome. Everyone is expected to label synthetic data and distinguish observed behavior from estimates.
